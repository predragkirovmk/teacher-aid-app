## In short, for students

This is the short version. The full version below is the one that applies.

- You use TeacherAid because your school and your teacher chose it. You pay nothing.
- You join a lesson with the QR code your teacher shows. Do not send the code to anyone who is not in the lesson.
- Answer by yourself. Do not pretend to be another student, and do not try to break the app.
- If you leave the quiz while a question is open, it is recorded and your teacher sees it. Nobody punishes you automatically. Your teacher decides your grades, not the app.
- Write your questions to your teacher politely. They are kept with your name.
- Your data and your rights are explained in the Privacy Policy. If something worries you, tell your teacher, your school or your parents.

7.4. **Questions to the teacher.** The Student may write a question to the teacher. It is kept with the student's name, only the teacher sees it, and it must be polite.

7.5. **Notifications.** If the Student turns notifications on, the browser gives us an address for sending notifications to that device. Notifications can be turned off at any time in the phone's or the browser's settings.
