Why it feels crowded

Measured on the app as it is today, in a 1280 by 800 laptop window, with the demo school's data.

  • 82%of the text is bold

    On the live lesson screen, 82% of the labels and lines of text are bold or semi-bold. On Lessons it is 79%. When everything is emphasised, nothing leads the eye.

  • 26buttons for 13 lessons

    Every lesson is its own box with two buttons, and clicking the box itself does nothing. The same pattern repeats on Today, Classes and the Library.

  • 4screens down

    That is where End lesson sits while a question is open. The busiest moment of a class has one of the longest pages: six panels stacked over 4.2 screens.

  • 6.9screens of scrolling

    A class's results put eight sections on one page. The lesson editor runs to 5.7 screens, with every question drawn as a stack of boxes.

  • 3shades of dark teal

    Sidebar, background and cards are three close teals held apart by white outlines. It reads as heavy, and a dark screen is harder to read on a laptop in a bright classroom.

The direction

Five rules, applied to every screen. The four drawings below show where the screens are heading. Step 1 gave them the colours and type and step 2 built the first drawing, Today; the other arrangements are built in steps 3 to 5.

  • One job per screen

    Each screen shows what you do now. The rest is one labelled click away, never hidden behind hover or a shortcut.

  • Lists, not stacks of boxes

    Rows on one calm surface. Click a row to open it. One button per row.

  • One accent

    Deep teal for the main action. Yellow only while a lesson is live, so it means something.

  • Type carries the hierarchy

    One large title, regular text, bold only for names and numbers. Nothing smaller than 16 px.

  • The live lesson fits one screen

    The question and the next step stay put. The class, the questions and End lesson sit beside them, not below.

The layout is the same in both. Only the colours differ. The projector screen stays dark teal either way.

What does not change

  • Every feature and every flow. Nothing is removed.
  • Macedonian first, English second, the same plain wording.
  • Right and wrong are an icon and a word, never colour alone. Red stays for End and warnings.
  • A word beside every icon. Nothing hidden behind hover, drag or shortcuts.
  • Work saves itself. Anything that cannot be undone asks first.
  • Readable contrast, keyboard use, 200% zoom and Reduce Motion, checked on every step.
  • The backend contract. No screen needs new data, so the developer's spec is untouched.
  • The student app and the website. This work is the teacher app only.
