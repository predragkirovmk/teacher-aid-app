## Design Context

### Users
Secondary-school students in Macedonia (about 12 to 18), on their own phones, one-handed,
often in a classroom with a teacher waiting. Jobs: scan the class QR to check in and join the
live quiz, answer quickly, then glance at attendance, points, schedule and alerts. They give
a screen a few seconds; nothing should need instructions. Macedonian first (Cyrillic, which
runs 20 to 30 % wider than Latin), English second.

### Brand Personality
Clear, warm, quick. A good teacher: relaxed authority with a sense of humour. Friendly
without being a kids' app, calm without being cold. Not Kahoot's carnival, not a school
admin portal.

### Aesthetic Direction
Version 2, blue and yellow (12 Oct 2026, Apple HIG pass 13 Oct). Source of truth:
`teacher-aid-flutter/design/teacheraid/DESIGN.md`; `app/(app)/app.css` is the reference
implementation.
- Blue is the ground, yellow is the answer: a saturated royal-blue field (`#1A5DAE` to
  `#134785`), flat blue cards (`#2468BE`), white type, and one yellow (`#FFC83D`) object per
  screen that says "do this now".
- Two hues only. Right and wrong are an icon plus a word, never green or red.
- Flat content cards and inset grouped lists. Glass only for things that float (tab bar,
  sheets, toasts). No moving background.
- Golos Text for titles and numbers, the system font for body text. Phosphor icons, regular
  weight, filled for the selected tab.
- 8pt spacing grid with 4pt half-steps. Large titles on tab roots.
- Motion: SwiftUI-sampled spring tokens, only where something changed. One rare reward moment
  per flow. The scan line is the only loop.
- Dark only: the blue ground stays, there is no light mode.
Anti-references: purple or cyan quiz apps, claymorphism, confetti everywhere, gradients on
content, glowing dark mode, emoji icons, decorative numbers or bars that show no real data.

### Design Principles
1. **The scan is the product.** The yellow Scan button is reachable from every tab root and
   is the one yellow control on screen.
2. **One thing per screen.** Each screen shows what the student needs to do or read now.
3. **Thumb-first.** Tap targets of at least 44 pt (56 pt for primary buttons), actions in
   the lower half, text that wraps instead of truncating at 200 % size.
4. **Honest data.** Every number, bar and badge is real. If it shows no data, delete it.
5. **Motion explains a change.** Springs for state changes and arrivals, nothing on
   ordinary screen loads, reduced motion keeps the fades and drops the movement.
